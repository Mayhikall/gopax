# Gopax: a travel journal

Direction chosen under the owner's request to redesign the UI using our judgment.
Audience: travelers recording tickets, reviewing estimated emissions, and claiming eligible rewards.
Reading: a practical travel journal with the visual language of printed tickets.
Dial: ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 2.

- Warm paper, dark green ink, and a restrained citron accent retain Gopax's ecological identity while separating actions from supporting content.
- A light paper theme is intentional: the product centers on reading receipts and travel records, including on phones outdoors.
- Manrope remains the locally hosted interface font for legible controls and numbers. Georgia gives introductory headlines the voice of a personal travel journal, without a new font request.
- The existing logo and transport icons remain. Transport, upload, wallet, and navigation icons identify their actual actions; decorative glyphs are removed.
- The existing route-and-leaf illustration appears only on the full impact summary, linking travel to its environmental comparison without implying a measured map.
- A ticket's perforation and origin/destination line form the repeated identity motif. No decorative background grids or fabricated maps.
- The dashboard prioritizes estimated savings and adding a ticket. Supporting balances sit beside that summary; journeys form a continuous ledger instead of a stack of floating cards.
- Surfaces group forms and trip details. Dividers group lists and metrics. Shadows are reserved for the sample paper ticket and sticky navigation separation.
- Spacing separates summary, actions, and history. Mobile stacks the summary and keeps the existing five destinations within thumb reach.
- Motion: a brief page entrance establishes navigation, a one-time ticket entrance introduces the sample, chart bars reveal proportions, and controls respond to hover/press. Reduced motion removes these transitions. No ambient animation loops; a spinner only indicates active loading.
- Copy names the action and explains estimates and eligibility. Existing fixtures are visibly marked as samples; live data and wallet flows retain their sources.
- Rounded corners distinguish controls (8px), panels (12px), and circular avatar placeholders. Status badges communicate real states.
- Existing routes and navigation destinations are preserved. Preview tools remain separate from product content.
