# Anti-slop copy audit 001

Date: 2026-09-20
Mode: AFTER
Scope: user-facing English copy across the landing page and authenticated shell
Framework: Next.js 16.3.4 with React 19
Styling: existing semantic class names in `src/app/globals.css`
Direction: `DESIGN.md`, practical travel journal with printed-ticket cues
Review boundary: copy density, repetition, usefulness, and the layout impact of removing text. Visual styling, motion playback, and interaction behavior were inspected only where copy removal could affect them.

## Coverage

| Category | Evidence inspected | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Typography | Landing headings, page descriptions, sidebar labels, footer copy, ticket captions | 5 copy findings |
| Surfaces | Landing header, sidebar, top bar, footer, profile card, sample ticket | 3 density findings |

## Findings

### 1. MEDIUM: show the network only where it affects an action

- Locations: `src/app/page.tsx:45`, `src/components/layout/app-shell.tsx:73`, `src/components/layout/app-shell.tsx:91`, `src/app/layout.tsx:13`
- Rule: R-09, R-31
- Current: `BSC Testnet` is repeated in the landing header, desktop sidebar, every authenticated top bar, and metadata.
- Change: remove those passive labels and remove the network name from the metadata description. Keep `BSC Testnet` in `src/features/profile/profile-screen.tsx:63` and `Switch to BSC Testnet` in `src/components/layout/app-shell.tsx:127`, where the network information helps the user inspect or correct wallet state.
- Reason: a persistent status-like badge that does not reflect the connected wallet adds noise. Network copy is useful at the point of diagnosis or action.

### 2. LOW: remove the app footer slogans

- Location: `src/components/layout/app-shell.tsx:136`
- Rule: R-05, R-16, C-3
- Current: `A record of the way you move.` and `Gopax · Testnet edition` repeat the journal theme and testnet label after every screen.
- Change: remove the entire `page-footer` block and its now-unused CSS.
- Reason: neither line adds navigation, status, or guidance.

### 3. LOW: remove brand-adjacent filler labels

- Locations: `src/app/page.tsx:44`, `src/components/layout/app-shell.tsx:49`, `src/components/layout/app-shell.tsx:76`, `src/app/layout.tsx:11`
- Rule: R-16, R-31
- Current: `A journal for the way you move.`, `Your travel journal`, `Made for the way you move.`, and the title `Gopax · Your travel journal` repeat the same positioning around an already visible Gopax brand.
- Change: remove the three visible labels. Shorten the default metadata title to `Gopax`.
- Reason: repetition does not explain the product more clearly and weakens the hierarchy around navigation and actions.

### 4. MEDIUM: remove the duplicate sidebar promotion

- Location: `src/components/layout/app-shell.tsx:64`
- Rule: R-05, C-3
- Current: `Got a ticket?`, its supporting sentence, and `Record a journey` duplicate the permanent `Add trip` navigation item directly above them.
- Change: remove the full `sidebar-note` block, its `TicketCheck` and `ArrowUpRight` imports if unused, and its now-unused CSS.
- Reason: the same action should not compete with itself in a narrow navigation column.

### 5. LOW: make the landing hero factual and shorter

- Location: `src/app/page.tsx:50`
- Rule: R-16
- Current: `Your ticket has more to tell.`, `A journey worth keeping.`, and `Give your everyday journeys a place to land.` use three metaphors before describing the product.
- Change: remove the eyebrow. Replace the heading with `Upload a ticket. See the estimated impact.` Replace the paragraph with `Save the route and claim GOPAX when the trip is eligible.`
- Reason: the revised copy reaches the real actions without inventing a larger promise.

### 6. LOW: remove decorative captions from the sample ticket

- Locations: `src/app/page.tsx:116`, `src/app/page.tsx:147`, `src/app/page.tsx:152`
- Rule: R-16, C-3
- Current: `A look inside your journal`, `Gopax travel record`, `01 / Train`, and `One ticket. A clearer picture of your travel.` surround a sample already labeled `Sample data` and `Sample ticket`.
- Change: keep one `Sample data` label, then remove the other decorative caption and the entire sample-ticket footer.
- Reason: the route, date, distance, and estimated savings already explain the sample.

### 7. LOW: reduce the landing explainer to information not stated above

- Location: `src/app/page.tsx:156`
- Rule: R-05, R-16, C-3
- Current: `Keep the ticket. Understand the trip.` and the following paragraph repeat the hero's upload, route, estimate, and reward message.
- Change: replace the section with concise support and caveat copy: `Train, bus, airplane, car, and motorcycle tickets are supported.` Keep `Estimates are not carbon offsets. Rewards depend on trip eligibility.`
- Reason: supported transport and estimate limitations add information. The second marketing summary does not.

### 8. LOW: remove empty descriptions from task-focused screens

- Locations: `src/features/impact/home-screen.tsx:37`, `src/features/trips/trip-detail.tsx:118`, `src/features/profile/profile-screen.tsx:34`, `src/app/onboarding/page.tsx:13`
- Rule: R-16
- Current: `Here’s where your journeys have taken you.`, `The story behind your journey.`, `Your Gopax travel companion`, `A little introduction`, and `Make yourself at home. Your next journey starts here.` do not clarify the adjacent data or form.
- Change: remove these lines and let the page title, trip data, profile name, and form label carry the hierarchy.
- Reason: task screens benefit from direct labels; mood copy between the heading and task only slows scanning.
